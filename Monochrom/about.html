ABOUT MONOCHROME

Who We Are

Monochrome is a Modeling agency that was founded by Blessed Khoza in 2026, the founder of the organization had a goal of uniting people of different races and so from the comfort of his home in Wasbank he started an Instagram page which he used to showcase the beauty of people as a whole and he achieved this by exclusively posting images in black and white, which in theory was meant to remove any bias regarding skin tone or complexion Monochrome has a clear mission, which is to unite all South Africans and help to eliminate any prejudice or negative stigmas surrounding skin color, showing people that there is beauty in everyone regardless of color, uniting everyone under the banner of black and white Monochrome is a modelling agency built around the belief that beauty exists in every person.

Our purpose is to provide models with opportunities to express themselves, develop their confidence and build professional careers within the modelling industry.


Our Philosophy

Black and white represent simplicity, elegance and timelessness. Our brand uses these two colours to represent the idea that beauty does not need to be defined by colour.

We welcome individuals regardless of race, skin tone, background or appearance. Every person has a unique form of beauty.


Our Vision

To create a modelling community where individuality is respected, diversity is celebrated and every model has the opportunity to be seen.
